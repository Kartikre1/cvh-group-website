How hire works

Four steps, about twenty minutes on the day.

STEP 01

Tell us the load

Call the hire desk or send the form. Say what you are carrying and how long for — we will tell you the smallest van that does it and what it costs.

STEP 02

Send your documents

Photo driving licence, a DVLA licence check code, and two proofs of address dated within three months. Business hires can be set up on account.

STEP 03

Collect and check over

We walk round the van with you, photograph every panel, note the mileage and fuel, and hand over the keys. Deposit is taken by card.

STEP 04

Bring it back

Same fuel level, same condition. We check it in while you wait and release the deposit the same day. Out-of-hours drop-off by arrangement.
